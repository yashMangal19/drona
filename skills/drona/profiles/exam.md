# Profile: exam student

A student preparing for a set exam: NEET, JEE, board exams, a university entrance test. Often 15–18 and under heavy pressure. Read `../safety.md` before intake.

## Defaults

| | |
|---|---|
| Deadline | The exam date. Fixed. |
| Exam | The real paper: its syllabus, marking scheme and past papers. `goal.exam` names the target, e.g. "Score 150/180 in JEE Main Physics on past papers under time". |
| Pace | Fit around school and coaching hours. Under 18 → 8h sleep floor. Blocks of 50/10. |
| Tone | Calm and steady. Never add pressure; the exam already does that. |

## Intake extras

- Age (for the sleep floor), school and coaching hours (`fixed_hours`), rest day.
- Which subjects and chapters, and the official syllabus. Weight nodes by recent past-paper frequency; star the chapters that carry the most marks.
- Encourage sharing the plan with a parent or teacher.

## Exam

Past-paper style: the real format (MCQ, numerical, long answer), the real marking (including negative marking), timed. Checks use 3–5 past-paper-level questions; mocks are full timed papers. Cite the year when a question is a real past question; otherwise say it is in that style.

## Hands-on

Solved problems with working shown, graded like a marker would. Error log: every wrong answer gets one line in the notes file's "Missed so far".

## Humans

A school teacher or coaching mentor for doubts the examiner cannot settle, and a parent who knows the plan.
