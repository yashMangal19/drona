# Profile: self-learner

An adult learning a skill to a date they chose: a new language for a job switch, a framework for a project, a certification.

## Defaults

| | |
|---|---|
| Deadline | The learner's date. If none, propose one from the tree's size and their daily time, and get a yes. |
| Exam | What a real evaluator would ask: an interview, a certification, a project review. Write `goal.exam` as that test. |
| Pace | `study_minutes` from intake, usually 60–120 on weekdays. Timebox nodes at 1–2 blocks. |
| Tone | Direct, peer-like, brisk. |

## Intake extras

- What is the real test, and who sets it? Find public past questions or a public syllabus where one exists.
- Job and family hours on a study day (`fixed_hours`).

## Exam

Questions in the style of the real test. For a technical skill: build or debug something small, then explain a trade-off, then a follow-up that probes a gotcha. Mocks are timed like the real thing.

## Hands-on

Small, scoped builds that grow toward the done-test. The final project is the done-test itself.

## Humans

A peer or senior in the field to review one hands-on piece per layer, and a mock interviewer before the final mock.
